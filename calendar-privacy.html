beb — Google Calendar privacy information

Last updated: 23 October 2026

beb is a private booking-management application used by the business’s authorized users.

The Google Calendar connection uses the calendar list to select an owned calendar and creates, updates, or removes beb booking and trial events in that calendar. Event information includes the customer name, service, event or trial date/time, and internal identifiers used to keep events synchronized. It does not send booking notes, prices, deposits, payment details, photos, or accounting documents to Google Calendar. Other events in the selected calendar are not managed by beb.

The app stores the connected Google account identifier, selected calendar identifiers, and encrypted authorization credentials needed for ongoing synchronization. Access to the business application is restricted to its configured authorized users.

Users can revoke the Google connection through their Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops future synchronization; it does not automatically delete existing events or booking records.
